World Athletics points are meant to make a 1200-point shot put worth the same as a 1200-point 100 m. I scraped 6,400 performances from the 2023 and 2024 top lists (top 100 per event, men and women), each with the points it was awarded, and compared the median of each event's 30 highest scores. Level tables would put those medians close together. They spread over 68.5 points, with running events at the top and jumps and throws at the bottom.

Every event against the norm

What it shows, and what it doesn't

An event's elite scores depend on the table's calibration and on how deep the current field is. A thinly contested event shows a lower ceiling even under a fair table. This study can't separate the two, so the claim is that equal points don't currently mean equal elite standing. It does not show the tables are miscoded.

The top 30 pools men and women, and two seasons is a short window. Checking each table against a fixed frontier, such as the world record, would be the next step.
